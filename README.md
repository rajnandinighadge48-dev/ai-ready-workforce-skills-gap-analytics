## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores workforce readiness for an AI-driven workplace. It focuses on workforce skills, AI tool proficiency, technical capabilities, learning agility, and employee development priorities.

The goal is to demonstrate how HR analytics can support data-informed workforce planning and skills development.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Assess overall workforce AI readiness.
- Identify employees and departments with higher skills gaps.
- Compare AI tool proficiency with technical skills.
- Explore training hours and learning agility.
- Analyze workforce performance, engagement, and job satisfaction.
- Support targeted employee learning and development planning.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains workforce attributes such as:

- Employee ID, department, and job role
- Age and years of experience
- Monthly salary
- Performance, productivity, and goal achievement
- Job satisfaction and employee engagement
- Training hours and overtime
- AI tools and technical skills scores
- Learning agility and AI readiness scores
- Skills gap level and future role interest

The dataset is intended for portfolio analysis. No real employee information should be published without authorization.

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Workforce Overview
Explore employee distribution, experience, performance, and engagement.

### 2. AI Readiness
Examine AI readiness scores and compare readiness across departments.

### 3. Skills Gap Analysis
Review skills gap categories and identify potential development priorities.

### 4. Training & Development
Explore training hours, technical skills, AI tool proficiency, and learning agility.

### 5. Department Insights
Compare workforce metrics across departments to support HR planning.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

The project can report the following KPIs after validation:

## 💡 Potential Business Value

The analysis can help HR teams:

- Prioritize learning and development initiatives
- Identify departments that may need additional support
- Understand workforce AI readiness
- Inform future workforce planning
- Build evidence-based employee development strategies

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Findings depend on the quality and completeness of the dataset.
- Relationships between training and readiness do not prove causation.
- Skills-gap categories and readiness thresholds should be validated against organizational standards.
- Results should be interpreted as analytical insights, not automatic employment decisions.
